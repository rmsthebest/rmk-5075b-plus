# Board facts

What the chip on the Akko 5075B Plus actually does, where that matters to the
simulator. The models follow these lines, not our wb32-pac or wb32-hal, and
not Renode where Renode disagrees. Each line names its source and the test
that pins it (`-` until the test is ported).

| Fact | Source | Test |
|---|---|---|
| TIM2 compare alarms fire only at the 16-bit wrap: a 1 ms alarm took 65.5 ms. Renode's `STM32_Timer` matches compares, which the chip doesn't. | Board, 2026-10-27: scan diagnostics over Vial 0xD1 (commit 9eef49c) | `tim2::tests::sixteen_bits_and_compare_at_wrap_only` |
| TIM2 is 16-bit: CNT, ARR and CCRx keep their low 16 bits. | ChibiOS `WB32_TIM2_IS_32BITS FALSE`; sim/akko.resc | `tim2::tests::sixteen_bits_and_compare_at_wrap_only` |
| Blank flash reads 0x00, not 0xFF. A DFU download leaves stale bytes past the end of a shorter image. | Board, 2026-10-26 (DFU readback) | - |
| The chip is a WB32FQ95xC: 256 KB flash, 36 KB SRAM, SYS_ID 0x3A50E980. | Board: wb32-dfu-updater_cli on download | `chip::tests` (sizes only) |
| Out of stop mode every clock is back at its reset value, USBPRE, USBCLKENR and the USB FIFO clock included. | RM2905025 5.3.4 | - |
| Stop entry is skipped while any EXTI request bit is pending. | RM2905025 5.4 | - |
| PWR_CFGR is reset only by power-on reset; so are PWR SR1, GPREG0 and GPREG1. | RM2905025 5.4.5-5.4.8 | `sysctl::tests::a_system_reset_keeps_pwr_sr1_gpreg_and_cfgr` |
| SYSRESETREQ keeps SRAM: the panic record and the bootloader magic at 0x2000_6FFC survive it. | Board: boot-guard's panic record survives the reset into the ROM bootloader and back (M3, tools/m3-check.py, 2026-10-26) | - |
| The core is a Cortex-M3 r2p0 (CPUID 0x412FC230) with 4 priority bits, an MPU and 37 IRQs (0-36, I2S last). | Vendor wb32fq95xx.h (`__CM3_REV`, `__NVIC_PRIO_BITS`, `__MPU_PRESENT`, IRQn) | `cortex-m3-emu` `Config`, `ppb.rs` |
| The ROM feeds the IWDG only while RCC+0x104 is non-zero. What sets it is unknown. | ROM disassembly, rom/notes.md. Open: read 0x40010D04 on the board after starting an IWDG | - |

| AIRCR reads with bits 14:11 clear (ARMv7-M: reserved, RAZ). Renode's reads with bit 13 set, which shows in the ROM's priority-grouping write (0x05FA2300 in Renode's traces). | ARM DDI 0403E B3.2.6; unconfirmed on the board | `rom_traces.rs` |
| SysTick: RVR = N-1 gives a period of N clocks; a CVR write clears it, and RVR loads on the next clock; a disabled counter doesn't count. Renode's model differs on all three: its period is RVR clocks, a CVR write reloads at once and runs about 3 clocks ahead, and the count starts at a CVR write even before ENABLE. akko-rmk's tickless time driver restarts SysTick twice a millisecond, so Renode's wakes drift about 7 cycles/ms early, which moves its LED frames against the m7 key presses (2026-10-29: probes from SRAM and the firmware's own CVR reads, identical instructions in both). | ARM DDI 0403E B3.3; unconfirmed on the board | `cpu.rs` `systick_*` |
| ICSR.RETTOBASE reads 0 in a handler while another exception is active (nested). Renode's reads 1 in SysTick preempting the QSPI interrupt: ChibiOS's `__port_irq_epilogue` then pushes a context-switch frame that is never popped, 32 bytes of the idle thread's stack per nested tick. In QMK, by the end of the first 2048-byte flash READ the stack has run over `spiStarted`, so `spi_stop` leaves CS low and the next command's bytes join the READ (Renode's `READ 0x000000 n=2050`). Renode's QMK then hangs on the flash where the golden traces end (2026-10-29: lockstep replay, `compare-renode qmk-via`). | ARM DDI 0403E B3.2.4; unconfirmed on the board | `cpu.rs` `rettobase_is_clear_in_a_nested_handler`, `qmk_reference.rs` |
| B1 (USB_POWER_EN) high switches D+'s pull-up off and the host loses the device; low, it's attached. The vendor raises it for wireless (`usb_power_disconnect`) and lowers it for USB (`usb_power_connect`). The host then holds SE0, which the device's controller sees as a bus reset (USB 2.0 7.1.7.3). With the cable out, the device's own pull-up holds J: an idle bus, a suspend after 3 ms (7.1.7.6). | Vendor 5075b_plus.c, config.h; USB 2.0 7.1.7; unconfirmed on the board | `board_wiring.rs` `wireless_mode_lifts_b1_...`, `the_cable_out_suspends_...` |
| A7 is high while the cable is in (VBUS); A15 (BAT_FULL_PIN, pulled up) is low while the charger charges, which is with the cable in and the battery not full. | Vendor config.h, 5075b_plus.c (`bat_full_flag = readPin(BAT_FULL_PIN)`); unconfirmed on the board | `parts::tests`, `board_wiring.rs` `the_charger_drives_a15_...` |
| A8 (LED_POWER_EN) powers the WS2812 chain: low, the LEDs are dark and take no data; powered again they start dark until a frame. | Vendor 5075b_plus.c; WS2812 power-on state; unconfirmed on the board | `ws2812::tests::unpowered_...`, `board_wiring.rs` `the_lighting_off_...` |
| The HSE crystal is 12 MHz, so QMK's PLL set-up (HSE / 2 x 16) gives 96 MHz, which its UART divider for 115200 (DLL 52, DLF 1) assumes, and that works on the board. | ChibiOS GENERIC_WB32_FQ95XX mcuconf.h `WB32_HSECLK`; board (QMK's wireless link works) | `qmk_reference.rs` `qmk_runs_at_96_mhz`, `m2_hal.rs` `the_clocks_are_96_mhz_after_start_up` |
| The clock tree: MAINCLK from MHSI after reset, switched by MAINCLKUEN only to a stable source; AHB, APB1 and APB2 each divide MAINCLK (RATIO + 2 while DIVEN), each APB off until its SRCEN; the PLL is its input (MHSI or HSE, through PLLPRE, which SRCEN gates) times 24, 20, 16 or 12. | RM2905025 7.2, 7.3, 11.4.11 | `sysctl::tests` |
| Ready flags rise a start-up time after the enable: MHSI 2 us, FHSI 0.5 us, LSI 85 us, the PLL 200 us after its input is stable (DS maximums); the HSE crystal's isn't given, 2 ms is assumed. | DS1905020 tables 5.2-6 to 5.2-9 | `sysctl::tests::qmks_pll_set_up_gives_96_mhz_once_locked` |
| Non-zero reset values: RCC AHBENR0 0x7B, AHBENR1 0x3D, AHBENR2 0x03, IWDGCLKENR 0x01; ANCTL MHSIENR and FHSIENR 1 (both stable); PWR CFGR 0x3BE. ANAKEY1/2 read 0. Firmware sets AHBENR1 bits with a read-modify-write, so the goldens recorded in Renode (whose RCC resets to 0) are compared with 0x3D or-ed in. | RM2905025 5.4.8-5.4.10, 7.3.11-7.3.16, 11.4.2-11.4.5 | `sysctl::tests::reset_values_and_the_keys_read_0`, `common::golden`, `rom_traces.rs` |
| The ROM bootloader runs at 48 MHz with APB2 off, and feeds USB MAINCLK undivided (USBPRE 0x08); the firmware's 96 MHz goes through USBPRE / 2 (0x09). | The ROM dump in the sim (rom/rust agrees); RM 7.3.5 | `rom_sim.rs` `the_rom_runs_at_48_mhz_with_apb2_off` |
| The core, SysTick (CLKSOURCE 1) and DWT CYCCNT run at HCLK; SysTick's external reference (CLKSOURCE 0) is HCLK/8. A clock switch carries both counters on from their values at the new rate. | RM2905025 7.2.3 (clock tree figure); ARM DDI 0403E B3.3 | `cpu.rs` `systick_external_reference_is_hclk_over_8`, `a_clock_change_rebases_systick_and_cyccnt`, `instructions_take_a_clock_of_the_bus_hclk` |
| The ROM's start-up disconnect is 15 SysTick periods of 4.8 M core clocks: 1.5 s at its 48 MHz (0.72 s in Renode's flat 100 MIPS). | rom/rust main.rs, clock.rs (from the dump) | `rom_traces.rs` (boot runs 1.78 s) |
| A peripheral's registers answer only while RCC clocks it: its enable bit (AHBENR1, APB1ENR, APB2ENR), for APB ones the bus clock (APBnPRE SRCEN) and the bus matrix (APB1ENR BMX1EN bit 15, APB2ENR BMX2EN bit 11), and its reset bit clear. Otherwise reads give 0 and writes are dropped (the sim reports `Diag::ClockOff`; what the chip does is a board check). A reset bit (AHBRSTR1, APB1RSTR, APB2RSTR) puts the peripheral back to its reset state. TIM2 counts PCLK1, and stops while gated. The ROM resets GPIOA-D (APB1RSTR 0x1E0) and USB (AHBRSTR1 bit 1) before handing over. | RM2905025 7.3.12-7.3.15, 7.3.21-7.3.23; the ROM dump | `chip.rs` `a_peripheral_answers_only_with_its_clock`, `a_reset_bit_holds_the_model_in_reset_then_releases_it`, `tim2_counts_pclk1_only_while_enabled`; `tim2::tests::it_counts_pclk1_and_stops_without_its_clock` |
| Instructions take the Cortex-M3 TRM's cycles with zero wait states: `asm::delay` (SUBS + a taken BNE) is 3 cycles a round, so `asm::delay(HCLK/1000)` takes 3 ms, as on the board (about 3000 TIM2 µs, 2026-10-27; m1b's `delay(4M)` blink at 8 MHz is 1.5 s). The flash cache leaves no wait states in a loop that small. The rest of the table (loads 2, pipelined loads and immediate-offset stores 1, LDM/STM 1+N, refill 1/2/3 after an immediate/register/loaded branch, multiply and divide early termination, unaligned +1/+2, exception entry 12, tail-chain 6, return 10) isn't checked on the board. | ARM DDI 0337G (Cortex-M3 TRM r2p0) Table 18-1, 18.3, 5.5-5.6 (`~/stuff/akko/arm/`); board | `cpu.rs` `cyccnt_counts_at_96_mhz`, `loads_and_stores_take_the_trms_cycles`, `branches_add_the_pipeline_refill`, `multiply_and_divide_terminate_early`, `exception_entry_tail_chain_and_return`; `boot_guard.rs` `caps_lock_led_blinks` |
| The SSIs shift a frame in DFS + 1 SCLK periods, SCLK = bus clock / SCKDV (QSPI PCLK1, SPIM2 PCLK2), only while SPIENR = 1 and SER selects a slave; TMOD 01 (transmit only) puts nothing in the RX FIFO. SPIM2's FIFOs hold 4 (its thresholds are 2 bits); QSPI's are said to hold 10 but TXFLR/RXFLR are 3 bits wide, so the sim keeps 8. Reset values: CR0 0x0100_0007, IER 0x1F, SR 0x06, so with its clock on and TXE set the interrupt line is high from reset (QMK's QSPI handler then runs once more and writes IER = 0 again). | RM2905025 22.2, 22.10.1-22.10.14, 23.2, 23.8.6-23.8.11 | `ssi::tests`, `m5_lighting.rs` `spim2_is_set_up_and_driven_as_qmk_does`, `qmk_reference.rs` `qmk_spi_flash_bring_up_matches_its_golden_trace` |
| The WS2812 latch is the line low for 280 us, counted on the wire: zero bytes and idle time alike (each SPI byte of the encoding ends low). | WS2812B datasheet (reset > 280 us) | `ws2812::tests::decodes_frames_and_catches_a_short_latch` |
| UART3 sends a character in 1 + WLS + parity + stop bits, each 16 periods of PCLK2 / (DLH:DLL + DLF/16): 86.8 us for QMK's 8N1 at DLL 52, DLF 1. With the FIFOs on (FCR[0] or SFE) each side holds 16 (the trigger levels 1/4/8/14 and 0/2/4/8 are the 16-deep DW 16550's; TFL/RFL are listed 4 bits wide), else 1. A byte arriving at a full RX FIFO is lost and sets LSR.OE. IIR priority: line status (0x6), received data (0x4) = character timeout (0xC, 4 characters of quiet), THR empty (0x2, cleared by an IIR read that reports it or a THR write). Resets: IIR 0x01, LSR 0x60, USR 0x06. | RM2905025 25.1.3, 25.2, table 25-2 | `uart::tests` |
| The FMC is busy (CON bit 7) for 2.1 ms a page program, 6.4 ms a page erase, 25.6 ms a mass erase. The ROM is in the flash macro, so nothing may be fetched or read from flash or the ROM meanwhile: the ROM starts an op and polls bit 7 from a routine it copies to SRAM. | DS1905020 "Flash memory characteristics" (tPROG, tERASE, tME); RM2905025 26.1; rom/notes.md | `rom_sim.rs` `a_page_is_erased_written_and_read_back` |
| The SPI NOR (assumed MX25L4006E, QMK's default part) holds WIP 1.4 ms a page program, 60 ms a 4 KB sector erase, 0.7 s a 64 KB block erase (typical figures). A command but RDSR during WIP is refused. | Macronix MX25L4006E figures as distributors list them (tPP, tSE, tBE) | `nor::tests::wip_lasts_the_parts_program_and_erase_times`, `m5_lighting.rs` `ee_clr_resets_the_saved_lighting` |
| SPIM2_MO is B15 on AF5; QSPI SCK/MI/MO are B3/B4/B5 on AF5; UART3 TX/RX are C10/C11 on AF7. A peripheral wired to a pin on another function sees nothing. | WB32FQ95 datasheet, alternate-function table | `ws2812::tests::b15_must_be_on_af5`, `nor::tests`, `radio::tests` |

## Open questions for the board

Things to read with Vial peek (0xDA) in an image that's being flashed anyway:
- Reset values of the registers the models assume (RCC, ANCTL, the GPIO MODER/PUPDR).
- SYS MEMSZ (0x4001_6404); rom/sim assumes 0x03.
- RCC+0x104 with and without the IWDG running.
- Flash wait states outside the cache: the delay loop with CACHE off, and with PREFEN 0 and 3; a loop with loads, and one with long branches (the sim assumes none, and the TRM's best-case refill).
- CPUID, to confirm r2p0.
- The SSI FIFO depths the HAL's probe finds (`ssi_fifo_depth`) for QSPI and SPIM2: the RM gives SPIM2 4 and QSPI 10 (with 3-bit level fields); the sim has 4 and 8. Also SSI CR0 and IER after reset (0x0100_0007, 0x1F in the RM).
- UART3's FIFO depth (16 from the trigger levels, but TFL/RFL are 4 bits) and whether a character arriving in stop mode, UART3's clock off, is lost without a trace, as the sim has it.
- The SPI NOR's part and its erase times: the sim takes the MX25L4006E's typical figures, and assumes 3.5 s for a chip erase (no figure found). Time a sector erase (WIP polls) in an image that's being flashed anyway; the maximum is about 4 times the typical.
- A read or write of a peripheral with its clock off (or held in reset): does it read 0 and drop the write, as the sim assumes, or fault?
- The HSE crystal's start-up time (ANCTL HSESR after HSECR0, timed with SysTick at MHSI); the sim assumes 2 ms.
- The radio module's timings (sim-rs `radio.rs`, assumed): a bonded select to CONNECTED (300 ms), a pairing (2 s), its 30 min sleep. Log them from the keyboard's side (the time between frames) in an image that's being flashed anyway.
- AIRCR (0xE000_ED0C), to confirm bit 13 reads 0.
